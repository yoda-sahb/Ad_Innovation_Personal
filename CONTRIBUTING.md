# Contributing

[Portfolio](README.md) · [Research method](docs/research-method.md)

Useful contributions make a hypothesis clearer, more testable, or easier to reject.

## What to contribute

- **Prior work:** a paper, standard, or implementation that addresses a specific part of a brief.
- **Counterexamples:** a realistic case where an assumption or proposed approach fails.
- **Evaluation designs:** a stronger baseline, useful measure, or smaller disconfirming test.
- **Documentation:** corrections, clearer definitions, or more precise scope.

## How to contribute

[Open an issue using the research feedback template](https://github.com/yoda-sahb/Ad_Innovation_Personal/issues/new?template=research-feedback.md). Identify the research direction, quote or link the claim being examined, and explain what your evidence would change.

For a documentation change, open a focused pull request explaining the problem and proposed revision. If a change introduces a new empirical claim, include a supporting source and its limitations.

## Keep claims proportionate to evidence

Label proposals and illustrative scenarios clearly. Do not present an untested hypothesis as a demonstrated capability or imply novelty without examining prior work.

Submit only material you are authorized to share publicly. This repository is for independent, company-agnostic research; confidential implementation details are outside its scope.
